Privacy

This website doesn't use cookies, advertising or tracking, and doesn't ask you to log in or give your name.

What stays on your device

  • If you pick Light or Dark at the bottom of the page, that choice is saved in your browser so it's remembered next time.
  • If you install the app, your phone saves copies of the pages so they open quickly and work without a signal.
  • Use my location asks your phone where you are and works out the nearest meetings on the phone itself. Your location isn't sent anywhere.

What's sent to other services

  • If you type a postcode to find your nearest meeting, that postcode is sent to postcodes.io, a free UK postcode service, to turn it into a map position.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles the technical details needed to deliver pages to you, such as your IP address.
  • Links to mocgb.net, the forum, Facebook, Instagram and maps take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
